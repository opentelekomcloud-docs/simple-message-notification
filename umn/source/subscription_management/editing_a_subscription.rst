Editing a Subscription
======================

Scenarios
---------

After a subscription is added, you can edit its information as required. Only the subscription description and filter policy can be edited.

Editing a Subscription on the Topic Details Page
------------------------------------------------

#. Log in to the management console.

#. In the upper left corner of the page, click |image1| and select the desired region and project.

#. Select **Application** > **Simple Message Notification**.

   The SMN console appears.

#. In the navigation pane, choose **Topics**.

   The **Topics** page appears.

#. Click the target topic name to go to the topic details page.


   .. figure:: /_static/images/en-us_image_0000002659277183.png
      :alt: **Figure 1** Topic details

      **Figure 1** Topic details

#. On the **Subscriptions** tab, view the subscriptions of the topic.

#. Locate the target subscription and click **Edit** in the **Operation** column, and edit the description or filter policy.


   .. figure:: /_static/images/en-us_image_0000002624951094.png
      :alt: **Figure 2** Editing a subscription

      **Figure 2** Editing a subscription

#. Click **OK** to complete the edit.

Editing a Subscription on the Subscription Page
-----------------------------------------------

#. Log in to the management console.

#. In the upper left corner of the page, click |image2| and select the desired region and project.

#. Select **Application** > **Simple Message Notification**.

   The SMN console appears.

#. In the navigation pane on the left, choose **Subscriptions**.

#. Locate the target subscription and click **Edit** in the **Operation** column, and edit the description or filter policy.


   .. figure:: /_static/images/en-us_image_0000002624951094.png
      :alt: **Figure 3** Editing a subscription

      **Figure 3** Editing a subscription

#. Click **OK** to complete the edit.
